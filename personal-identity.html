Practice
statement.

01

Agency / norms

My practice explores how individual agency is negotiated within social norms. We learn what is considered appropriate, adjust how we present ourselves across different contexts, and gradually internalise these expectations as forms of self-discipline.

02

Body / clothing

Clothing, movement and silhouettes are treated as visible negotiations between how a person feels and how they are expected to appear.

03

Material / image

Materials, objects and self-presentation return as feedback: they can hold social pressure, invite resistance and open space for choice, deviation and redefinition.
